Academic background & experience

I am Aruni Thiloka Ranawaka, an English language educator and academic with five years of experience in higher education and lecturing, supported by a strong academic background in English language education and translation studies. I hold a Master of Arts in Teaching English as a Second Language (M.A. TESL) from the University of Sri Jayewardenepura and a Bachelor of Arts (Honours) in Translation Studies with First Class Honours from the University of Kelaniya.

Throughout my academic journey, I have had the opportunity to work with learners from diverse educational and professional backgrounds. My teaching experience includes working as a Visiting Lecturer in English at the Advanced Technological Institute (ATI), Anuradhapura, and contributing to English language education in higher education and professional training contexts.

Academic qualifications

  • M.A. in Teaching English as a Second Language (TESL) — University of Sri Jayewardenepura
  • B.A. (Hons) in Translation Studies — First Class Honours, University of Kelaniya
  • Postgraduate research: The Impact of Technology-Assisted Language Learning on Academic Writing at the Post-Secondary Level — University of Sri Jayewardenepura
  • Specialised knowledge: ELT, TESL, Translation Studies, Academic Writing & Technology-Assisted Language Learning (TALL)

Professional experience

  • Lecturer / Visiting Lecturer in English — Higher Education
  • Visiting Lecturer in English — Advanced Technological Institute (ATI), Anuradhapura
  • Visiting Lecturer in English — The Open University of Sri Lanka
  • English Language Educator — professional & academic training programmes
  • Five years of lecturing experience in higher education

My teaching philosophy is founded on a simple belief:

Every learner has the potential to succeed when given the right guidance, encouragement, and opportunities to learn.

At Thiloka English Academy, I focus on developing practical communication skills, academic English, confidence, critical thinking, and effective language use. I believe that learning English should not be limited to memorising grammar rules. It should enable learners to communicate confidently, express ideas clearly, succeed academically, and prepare themselves for professional opportunities.
